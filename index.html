All Done?

Returning Your Thing

Three quick steps to send your item back to the ESC.

Repack everything

Place all pieces back in the bins, bags, or boxes they arrived in. Some items include their own re-packaging instructions.

Submit a transfer request

Complete a Transfer Request Form to send the item back to the ESC. In the comments, write: "Attention: Nick Schuster or Alison Pukurdpol."

Report any issues

Anything damaged, lost, or weird? Email nschuster@lps.k12.co.us so the next borrower gets a complete kit.
